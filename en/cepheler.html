02 / KEY FRONTSEVENTS & TURNING POINTS

THREE CONTINENTS, SIX BATTLES

The war's fronts.

Six battles that changed the course of the war, from Europe to the Pacific and from North Africa to the Eastern Front. Click a card for details.

PHOTO · 1940
WESTERN EUROPE · AIRJULY — OCTOBER 1940

Battle of Britain

The German air force, the Luftwaffe, tried to win control of the skies in preparation for an invasion of Britain. The Royal Air Force's resistance defeated the plan and forced Germany to postpone the invasion.

PHOTO · 1942
PACIFIC4 — 7 JUNE 1942

Battle of Midway

Having broken Japanese codes, the US Navy learned of the attack in advance and sank four Japanese aircraft carriers. With this victory, the advantage in the Pacific began to shift to the United States.

EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
PHOTO · 1942
NORTH AFRICAOCTOBER — NOVEMBER 1942

Battle of El Alamein

The British Eighth Army under Montgomery stopped Rommel's German-Italian forces in Egypt and forced them to retreat. The threat to the Suez Canal was removed.

EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
PHOTO · 1943
EASTERN FRONT1942 — 1943

Battle of Stalingrad

Remembered for its brutal street fighting and heavy losses, one of the decisive turning points on the Eastern Front. With the encirclement and surrender of the German Sixth Army, the advantage passed to the Soviets.

EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
PHOTO · 1944
WESTERN EUROPE6 JUNE 1944

Normandy Landings

The operation in which the Allies opened a new front in France and sped up the liberation of Western Europe. It is known as the largest seaborne invasion in history.

EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
PHOTO · 1945
THE END IN EUROPEAPRIL — MAY 1945

Fall of Berlin

The last days of the war in Europe. After the Soviet army captured Berlin, Nazi Germany surrendered unconditionally.
