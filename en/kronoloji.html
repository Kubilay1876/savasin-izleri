03 / TIMELINE1939 TO 1945

SIX YEARS, COUNTLESS TURNING POINTS

In the footsteps of time.

The events that changed the course of the war, year by year. Use the menu below to jump straight to a year.

1939 1940 1941 1942 1943 1944 1945

  1. 1939

    1 SEPTEMBER

    Invasion of Poland

    Germany's attack on Poland started the Second World War in Europe. Two days later, Britain and France declared war on Germany.

    17 SEPTEMBER

    The Soviets invade from the east

    Following the secret protocol of its non-aggression pact with Germany, the Soviet Union invaded Poland from the east.

  2. 1940

    10 MAY — 22 JUNE

    The fall of France

    Germany attacked the Netherlands, Belgium and France. Trapped Allied troops were evacuated from Dunkirk, and France was forced to sign an armistice on 22 June.

    JULY — OCTOBER

    Battle of Britain

    The Luftwaffe failed to win the air superiority needed to invade Britain. Germany postponed its invasion plans, while the bombing of cities (the Blitz) continued until spring 1941.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →

  3. 1941

    22 JUNE

    Operation Barbarossa

    Breaking the non-aggression pact, Germany invaded the Soviet Union. The largest military invasion in history opened the Eastern Front.

    7 DECEMBER

    Pearl Harbor

    Japan launched a surprise attack on the US naval base in Hawaii. The next day the United States entered the war, which became truly global.

  4. 1942

    4 — 7 JUNE

    Battle of Midway

    The US Navy sank four Japanese aircraft carriers. The advantage in the Pacific began to shift to the United States.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
    23 OCTOBER — 11 NOVEMBER

    El Alamein

    The British Eighth Army defeated Rommel's forces in Egypt, and the Axis retreat in North Africa began.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →

  5. 1943

    2 FEBRUARY

    Surrender at Stalingrad

    The remaining forces of the German Sixth Army surrendered, marking a major turning point on the Eastern Front.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
    5 JULY — 23 AUGUST

    Battle of Kursk

    Germany's last major offensive on the Eastern Front saw one of the largest tank battles in history. After the Soviet victory, the Red Army began to advance west.

    8 SEPTEMBER

    Italy surrenders

    After the Allied landing in Sicily and the fall of Mussolini, Italy signed an armistice with the Allies.

  6. 1944

    6 JUNE

    D-Day: Normandy

    The Allies landed on the coast of France and began their advance through Western Europe.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
    25 AUGUST

    Liberation of Paris

    Allied forces and the French Resistance entered Paris, ending four years of German occupation in the city.

  7. 1945

    4 — 11 FEBRUARY

    Yalta Conference

    Roosevelt, Churchill and Stalin discussed the post-war order in Europe and Germany's zones of occupation.

    8 MAY

    The war in Europe ends

    After the fall of Berlin, Germany surrendered unconditionally. 8 May is remembered as Victory in Europe Day.

    EXPLORE THE BATTLE →
    6 AND 9 AUGUST

    Hiroshima and Nagasaki

    The United States dropped atomic bombs on the Japanese cities of Hiroshima and Nagasaki. Tens of thousands of civilians died instantly, and thousands more in the following months from the effects of radiation.

    2 SEPTEMBER

    The official end of the war

    With Japan signing the instrument of surrender aboard the battleship USS Missouri, the Second World War officially came to an end.
