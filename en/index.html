MORE THAN A WAR

A world
reshaped.

The war that began in Europe with Germany's invasion of Poland on 1 September 1939 soon became a global conflict spreading across several continents.

By the time it ended in 1945, borders, international relations and the lives of millions had changed forever. This digital exhibition looks at both the military and the human side of the war.
